Mic tips

Use one ringing string, mute the others, keep the room quiet, and move the guitar closer if the app says the signal is too quiet.

Privacy

The microphone stream is analyzed in this browser tab. The app has no backend and does not upload or store raw audio.

Accuracy notes

Phone and laptop microphones vary. Use calibration when matching another tuner, piano, or rehearsal room reference.
